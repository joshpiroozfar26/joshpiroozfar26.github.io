Our story

A little café with a big kettle

Hearth & Honey opened in 2019 in an old saddler's shop on Fernley Bridge High Street. We wanted somewhere that felt like a friend's kitchen: mismatched chairs, a wood burner in winter and the smell of fresh bread from 7am.

Our coffee comes from a small roastery twelve miles away, our milk from a family dairy just outside town, and everything on the counter is baked here, by hand, that morning.

FAQs

Good to know

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If we think you need a scan or to see a specialist, we'll tell you and write to your GP.

Can I claim on health insurance?

We're recognised by most major UK health insurers. Bring your authorisation code to your first session and we'll invoice them directly where possible.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable you can move in, like shorts and a vest top. We may need to see the area we're treating.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need between three and six. We'll give you an honest estimate after your assessment and never book sessions you don't need.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. Late cancellations are charged at 50%.

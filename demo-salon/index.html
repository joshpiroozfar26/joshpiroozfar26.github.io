Welcome

Slow beauty, by the sea

Saltwater opened in a converted net loft on Chesilcombe harbour in 2017. We take our time: every appointment starts with a proper consultation and a good cup of tea.

We use vegan, cruelty-free colour, and our stylists train every season so you leave with something that suits you, not just what's trending.

FAQs

Questions local owners ask

Can't see yours? Just ask, I'm happy to help.

How long does it take?

Starter sites are usually live within 5–7 days, and Business sites within 7–14 days. The biggest factor is how quickly I get your text, photos and logo, and I can help write the words if you're stuck.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once it's paid for, the website, the content and your domain name are 100% yours. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, I'll hand over all the files.

What about hosting and my domain?

With the Care Plan, hosting and security are included and I handle the technical side. Without it, I can set the site up on a hosting account in your name. Domain names (e.g. yourbusiness.co.uk) are registered in your name and usually cost around £10–£15 a year, paid directly to the registrar.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start once you've approved the free mock-up, and the remaining 50% when you're happy and the site goes live. The Care Plan is billed monthly and you can cancel any time. Bank transfer or card accepted.

Can I make edits after it's live?

Yes. On the Care Plan, small monthly edits (prices, opening hours, menus, photos, new text) are included, so just send them over. Without it, edits are charged at a simple fixed rate, quoted before I start.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site is built to be fast and search-friendly. The Business package adds basic SEO set-up and Google Search Console, and I'll link it to your Google Business Profile so you show up in local searches and on Maps.

I'm not techy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what I'm here for. No jargon, no logins to wrestle with: you tell me what you want, and I sort it.
